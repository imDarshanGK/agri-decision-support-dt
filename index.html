Methodology

A tuned tree, not a guess

Most crop recommenders stop at "highest accuracy." This one is optimized, ranked, and explained end to end — each stage exists because the one before it wasn't enough on its own.

01

Baseline tree

A default Decision Tree, unmodified hyperparameters — the number every later stage has to beat.

02

Grid-search tuning

Depth, split thresholds, leaf size, and pruning tuned via cross-validated grid search.

03

Suitability ranking

Class probabilities ranked, not thresholded — you see the top alternatives, not just the winner.

04

Explainable rules

Feature importance and extracted rules turn the tree's splits into readable conditions.

05

Decision support

Served live below — soil and climate readings in, ranked and reasoned recommendations out.
